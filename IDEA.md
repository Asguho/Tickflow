**Tickflow** is an Android app that automatically tracks your workday using your office Wi-Fi connection. When you arrive at work, Tickflow starts tracking in the background and vibrates once you’ve worked your target time — for example eight hours. If you leave early or stay late, the difference is automatically carried over to the next day, so your reminders continuously adjust based on your real hours worked. You can also manually start or stop tracking outside the office for remote work, meetings, or travel. If Tickflow detects that you left the office and later returned, you can retroactively mark that time as work with a single tap, making it easy to keep your hours accurate without micromanaging timers or timesheets.
